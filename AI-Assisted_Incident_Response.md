# AI-Assisted Incident Response

## 1. Use AI For:

### Incident Summarization
- AI condenses large volumes of logs, alerts, and evidence into concise summaries.  
- Provides quick overviews for analysts and executives.  
- Reduces time spent manually parsing data.

### Threat Analysis
- AI correlates indicators with global threat intelligence feeds.  
- Identifies attacker TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, Procedures).  
- Suggests likely adversary groups or malware families.  

### Investigation Assistance
- AI highlights anomalies in logs and telemetry.  
- Suggests potential attack vectors and lateral movement paths.  
- Provides contextual insights (e.g., mapping to MITRE ATT&CK).  

### Report Drafting
- AI auto‑generates structured incident reports.  
- Ensures consistency with organizational templates.  
- Translates technical findings into business‑friendly language.  

### IOC Enrichment
- AI enriches raw Indicators of Compromise (IPs, hashes, domains).  
- Adds metadata: reputation scores, geolocation, hosting provider, related campaigns.  
- Helps analysts prioritize which IOCs are most dangerous.  

---

## 2. Validate Outputs

### Verify Findings
- Analysts must cross‑check AI conclusions with raw logs and threat intel.  
- Avoid blind trust — AI can misclassify benign events.  

### Review Evidence
- Ensure collected evidence supports AI’s analysis.  
- Validate forensic artifacts (PCAPs, hashes, registry changes).  

### Confirm Conclusions
- Analysts finalize incident classification and severity.  
- AI provides speed, but human judgment ensures accuracy.  

---

## Quick Summary
- **AI accelerates incident response**: summarization, analysis, investigation, reporting, IOC enrichment.  
- **Human analysts validate outputs**: verify findings, review evidence, confirm conclusions.  
- Together, AI + SOC analysts create a **faster, smarter, more resilient response cycle**.
