# SOC Analyst Responsibilities

## 1. Security Monitoring
SOC analysts are the **eyes of the organization**, continuously monitoring systems and networks for suspicious activity.  
Key tasks:
- Observe SIEM dashboards, IDS/IPS alerts, and endpoint telemetry.
- Track logs from servers, applications, firewalls, and cloud services.
- Detect anomalies such as unusual login attempts, privilege escalations, or abnormal traffic flows.
- Ensure 24/7 vigilance to catch threats early.

---

## 2. Alert Triage
Not all alerts are equal — analysts must **separate noise from real threats**.  
Responsibilities:
- Validate alerts to confirm if they are false positives or genuine issues.
- Prioritize alerts based on severity, asset criticality, and potential impact.
- Apply playbooks and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for consistent triage.
- Escalate only meaningful alerts to higher tiers, reducing analyst fatigue.

---

## 3. Incident Investigation
When an alert is deemed suspicious, analysts perform **deep investigation**.  
Steps:
- Collect evidence from logs, packet captures, and endpoint data.
- Correlate events across multiple sources (e.g., login attempts + malware detection).
- Identify root cause and attack vector.
- Assess scope: which systems, users, or data are affected.
- Document findings for response teams.

---

## 4. Escalation Workflows
SOC analysts follow structured **escalation paths** to ensure incidents are handled efficiently.  
Workflow:
1. **Tier 1 Analyst** → Initial triage and validation.  
2. **Tier 2 Analyst** → Deeper investigation, containment strategies.  
3. **Tier 3 Specialist** → Advanced forensics, malware reverse engineering, threat hunting.  
4. **SOC Manager** → Decision-making, communication with executives, compliance reporting.  

Escalation ensures that complex incidents reach the right expertise quickly.

---

## 5. Documentation and Reporting
Accurate documentation is critical for **learning and compliance**.  
Responsibilities:
- Record incident details: timeline, affected assets, actions taken.
- Maintain incident tickets in SIEM/SOAR platforms.
- Generate daily/weekly reports on alerts, incidents, and trends.
- Provide evidence for audits, regulatory compliance, and post-incident reviews.
- Share lessons learned to improve detection rules and playbooks.

---

## Quick Summary
- **Monitoring** → Watch systems continuously.  
- **Triage** → Filter noise, prioritize real threats.  
- **Investigation** → Analyze suspicious activity deeply.  
- **Escalation** → Route incidents to the right expertise.  
- **Documentation** → Record, report, and improve processes.  

SOC analysts are the **frontline defenders**, ensuring threats are detected, investigated, and escalated with precision.
