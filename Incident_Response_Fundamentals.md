# Incident Response Fundamentals

## 1. Preparation
Preparation is the foundation of incident response.  
Key actions:
- Build and train the Incident Response Team (IRT).  
- Define policies, playbooks, and escalation procedures.  
- Deploy monitoring tools (SIEM, EDR, IDS/IPS).  
- Establish communication channels with IT, legal, and management.  
- Conduct tabletop exercises and simulations.  

Goal → Ensure readiness before incidents occur.

---

## 2. Identification
This phase focuses on detecting and confirming suspicious activity.  
Steps:
- Monitor logs, alerts, and user reports.  
- Validate whether activity is malicious or benign.  
- Classify incident type (malware, phishing, insider threat, data breach).  
- Assess scope and potential impact.  

Goal → Recognize incidents quickly and accurately.

---

## 3. Containment
Containment prevents further damage while keeping business operations running.  
Types:
- **Short-term containment** → Isolate affected systems immediately.  
- **Long-term containment** → Apply patches, block malicious IPs, disable compromised accounts.  

Goal → Stop the spread of the attack without disrupting critical services.

---

## 4. Eradication
Eradication removes the root cause of the incident.  
Actions:
- Delete malware, malicious accounts, or unauthorized access.  
- Patch exploited vulnerabilities.  
- Correct misconfigurations.  
- Ensure no remnants of the attack remain.  

Goal → Eliminate the threat completely.

---

## 5. Recovery
Recovery restores systems to normal operation.  
Steps:
- Restore from clean backups.  
- Gradually bring services back online.  
- Monitor closely for reinfection or anomalies.  
- Validate system integrity before resuming full operations.  

Goal → Safely return to business as usual.

---

## 6. Lessons Learned
Post-incident review strengthens future defenses.  
Tasks:
- Document incident timeline, impact, and response actions.  
- Conduct debrief sessions with the response team.  
- Update detection rules, playbooks, and security controls.  
- Share findings with stakeholders and improve training.  

Goal → Continuous improvement and resilience.

---

## Quick Summary
- **Preparation** → Build readiness.  
- **Identification** → Detect and confirm incidents.  
- **Containment** → Limit damage.  
- **Eradication** → Remove the threat.  
- **Recovery** → Resume operations safely.  
- **Lessons Learned** → Improve future response.  

Incident response is a **cycle**: prepare → detect → contain → eradicate → recover → learn → improve.
