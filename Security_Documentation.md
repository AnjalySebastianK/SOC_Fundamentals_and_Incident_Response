# Security Documentation

## 1. Incident Reports
Incident reports are formal records of confirmed security incidents.  
Contents:
- Incident summary (who, what, when, where, why).  
- Timeline of events and response actions.  
- Systems, users, and data affected.  
- Containment, eradication, and recovery steps taken.  
- Impact assessment (business, financial, regulatory).  

Purpose → Provide evidence, accountability, and lessons learned.

---

## 2. Investigation Reports
Investigation reports document the **analysis process** behind an incident.  
Contents:
- Alert validation details.  
- Evidence collected (logs, PCAPs, forensic data).  
- Investigation workflow (scope, correlation, hypothesis testing).  
- Root cause analysis findings.  
- Recommendations for prevention.  

Purpose → Show how analysts reached conclusions and justify response actions.

---

## 3. Escalation Notes
Escalation notes are **handover documents** used when incidents move between tiers or teams.  
Contents:
- Alert details and validation status.  
- Evidence summary.  
- Actions already taken.  
- Pending tasks or unanswered questions.  
- Escalation reason (complexity, severity, expertise required).  

Purpose → Ensure smooth transition and avoid duplicated effort.

---

## 4. Case Management
Case management is the structured handling of incidents across their lifecycle.  
Components:
- Ticketing system (SIEM/SOAR platforms).  
- Case ID for tracking.  
- Assigned analysts and escalation path.  
- Status updates (open, investigating, contained, closed).  
- Linked evidence and documentation.  

Purpose → Maintain organization, accountability, and visibility across the SOC.

---

## 5. Audit Readiness
Audit readiness ensures documentation meets compliance and regulatory standards.  
Requirements:
- Complete and accurate incident records.  
- Evidence integrity (hashing, chain of custody).  
- Reports aligned with frameworks (ISO 27001, NIST, GDPR, HIPAA).  
- Demonstrated response timelines and effectiveness.  
- Lessons learned integrated into security policies.  

Purpose → Prove due diligence, reduce liability, and strengthen trust with regulators and stakeholders.

---

## Quick Summary
- **Incident Reports** → Record confirmed incidents.  
- **Investigation Reports** → Document analysis process.  
- **Escalation Notes** → Smooth handover between teams.  
- **Case Management** → Organized tracking of incidents.  
- **Audit Readiness** → Compliance and accountability.  

Security documentation is the **backbone of SOC transparency**, ensuring incidents are tracked, analyzed, escalated, and reported in a way that supports both operational efficiency and regulatory compliance.
